# Proposal

Step 7: Tickets and trip wallet

- **Demo act:** Act 3 (the fan after purchase, and the door: a live rotating QR scanned on a phone, then a screenshot of it rejected).
- **Features:** F2 trip wallet (Real), F3 rotating QR ticket (Real, headline), F4 Apple and Google Wallet pass preview (Thin), F5 personalise and check in online (Thin), F8 fan account (Thin), G2 door scanning page (Real, raised in review).
- **Decisions:** O5 (rotating QR with Ed25519-signed tokens), built on P3 (outbox and Redis Streams), U1 (JWTs verified by Go, guest claim), U2 (organiser separation), O3 (order and payment states), O6 (wallet ledger, for the paid late check-in).

## Why

After step 6 a fan can buy, but the purchase ends at a status page: there is no ticket, nothing to show at the gate and no way for the organiser to let people in. NIBIRU today issues static barcodes and PDFs (teardown section 5) that can be screenshotted and sold twice, and its success page shows "see you soon" before any ticket exists (section 10). Act 3 needs real tickets that are issued only after payment is confirmed, change every 15 seconds, and are checked by a door phone that keeps working without a network.

## What Changes

- The worker issues one ticket per paid unit from the `order.paid` event, idempotently, and emits `ticket.issued`. No event, no ticket.
- Each ticket has a generation number. Its live code changes every 15 seconds and carries the event, ticket, generation and time window, signed with Ed25519. Only the holder can fetch codes, as a short in-memory buffer.
- The signing key is generated by CI into a Kubernetes secret; the public keys are published for door devices. Reissuing a ticket (later: transfer, resale, upgrade) bumps the generation and adds the old one to a per-event revocation feed.
- A door scanning page for organiser staff verifies codes offline with the public key, rejects screenshots and old codes, prevents double entry, and syncs scans and revocations when online.
- A trip wallet: one timeline per fan with tickets, add-ons, tables, slots and transport, each with its next action and deadline (personalise, pick the day, check in, pay the next instalment, show the code).
- Thin: personalisation with a deadline, online check-in with a paid late check-in, pick-the-day-later passes, a signed ticket link for guests, a wallet pass preview, and a fan account that lists orders, tickets and instalment schedules.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
- `tickets`: issuing from confirmed payments, ticket generations, the rotating signed code, signing keys and the revocation feed, personalisation, online check-in, day picking, signed guest links and the wallet pass preview.
- `trip-wallet`: the fan's timeline across events and organisers, with next actions and deadlines.
- `fan-account`: orders, tickets and instalment schedules in one place.
- `door-scanning`: the offline door page that verifies rotating codes and records entries.

### Modified Capabilities
- None.

## Impact

- **Go (services/):** a `tickets` package in the core API (codes, revocation feed, personalisation, check-in), issuing in the worker from the `order.paid` stream, a scan-sync endpoint; new tables in the sales schema (tickets, ticket_generations, scans, revocations) under row-level security.
- **Next.js (apps/web):** `/[locale]/wallet` (trip wallet), `/[locale]/wallet/tickets/[id]` (live code), `/[locale]/account` (fan account), `/[locale]/t/[signed]` (guest link), `/door/[event]` (installable, offline door page with a service worker).
- **Contract:** new paths in `api/openapi.yaml` (tickets, codes, keys, revocations, scans).
- **Secrets:** an Ed25519 ticket-signing key generated by CI (`TICKET_SIGNING_KEY`, key id), private half only in the core API's Kubernetes secret.
- **Dependencies:** possibly two small browser utilities (a QR encoder and decoder, and an Ed25519 fallback where WebCrypto lacks Ed25519); see design.md.

# EPS Prototype

A prototype of a next-generation, white-label ticketing platform that any event organiser can use, built for the NIBIRU / EPS (Event Platform Systems) CTO challenge.

## Deliverables
1. **Prototype**: a working build, delivered within 48 hours.
2. **Demo video**: at most 5 minutes, showing the prototype and the architecture.
3. **Vision**: at most one page, covering what "next-gen" means, the first 6 months, a 100k-people-in-10-minutes on-sale, and the team.

## Contents
- `docs/research/`: the NIBIRU/EPS teardown, six competitor reports (`competitors/`) and `feature-matrix.md` (combined matrix and standout shortlist).
